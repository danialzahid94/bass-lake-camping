⚠️ Important Reminders

🌙 Quiet Hours
11 PM to 7 AM — Keep voices low and music off. Fines possible for violations.
🐻 Food Storage
NEVER store food in tents — raccoons and bears. Keep all food in cars or bear-proof containers.
🔥 Fire Safety
Campfires must be fully extinguished before leaving or sleeping. Use water, not dirt.
🚿 Comfort Stations
Flush toilets, hot showers, laundry available. Bring quarters for laundry machines.
❄️ May Weather
Daytime 15–20°C, nighttime 3–5°C. Bring warm layers, especially for evenings.
📱 Cell Reception
Spotty signal at camp. Download offline maps and emergency contacts before arriving.
🐕 Dogs
Dogs allowed on leash, NOT permitted on beaches. Keep them with you at all times.
✅ Check-in/Out
Check-in: 2 PM | Check-out: 2 PM Sunday. Aim to arrive by 11–12 PM Saturday — early arrival is fine, no gatehouse check-in needed.

🛒 Grocery Tips

Buy perishables Friday or Saturday morning before leaving Milton. Pack coolers with ice carefully — keep raw meat away from ready-to-eat food. Bring extra ice for drinks. Label food with names if shared. Don't forget condiments, oils, salt, pepper!
